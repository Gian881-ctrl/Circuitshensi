    How it works

    From the first symptom
    to the handover test.

    You decide after diagnosis. Here’s what we need from you, what we do at the bench, and what you receive when the work is complete.

    1. Request & quick assessment

      Send the model, symptoms, and any previous repair attempts. For a PCB, describe the brief and the files you already have. We reply with the next check and arrange drop-off or mail-in if the item needs bench time.

    2. Full diagnostics & transparent quote

      We check the fault and explain the proposed fix, required parts, total price, and estimated schedule. You approve the work before it begins. If the scope changes, we come back to you for a decision.

    3. Precision repair & stress testing

      We carry out the approved repair and repeat the conditions that exposed the fault. That can mean repeated mouse clicks, multiple charging connections, or a board check under its intended load. The test follows your device and the work agreed.

    4. Handover & warranty protection

      We explain what changed, how it tested, and how to collect it or arrange return delivery. Keep the repair summary, warranty dates, and reference together so we can pick up the same case if you need support.

    Featured work

    What a successful fix looks like.

    These three illustrative bench walkthroughs explain the repair decisions and the result we would check. The shop’s own video follows the examples.

    A phone that only charges at an angle

    The challenge
    The charging icon drops out when the cable moves. Buying a new port before checking the cable could leave the original problem untouched.
    The solution
    Try a known-good cable and power source, inspect the connector, and check the board connection. Quote port rework if the inspection confirms the fault there.
    The result to verify
    The phone holds a charging connection through repeated unplug-and-reconnect tests without pressure on the cable.

    A lifted connector pad on a board

    The challenge
    A connector has pulled away from its pad. Soldering it back in place alone can leave the signal path open.
    The solution
    Trace the affected path to an intact connection, inspect neighboring pads, and scope reconstruction of the damaged connection.
    The result to verify
    The repaired path has continuity, adjacent lines remain isolated, and the port works with a known-good accessory.

    A mouse that double-clicks on one press

    The challenge
    A single click opens files twice, yet the fault only appears intermittently. The symptom needs to be reproduced before opening the mouse.
    The solution
    Compare physical presses with registered clicks, isolate the affected button, and check switch compatibility before quoting a replacement.
    The result to verify
    Single presses register once, click-and-drag holds without dropping, and the other buttons still respond.

    Frequently asked questions

    Before you send it in.

    What will I pay, and when do I approve the repair?

    The estimator gives you a planning range for the issue you select. A bench diagnostic costs ₱300 and is credited toward the final repair bill when you proceed. After assessment we confirm the proposed parts, labor, and any courier charges. You can accept or decline before repair work starts.

    How long will the work take?

    As a guide, allow 1–5 working days for phones, 2–7 for board repairs, and 1–3 for keyboards or mice. PCB layout or assembly can take 3–10 working days. Bench windows start once the job is approved and the required parts or boards are available. Fabrication and courier transit add time. If you have a capstone deadline, include the date in your inquiry.

    What does the two-week warranty cover?

    The warranty applies to the completed repair described in your service confirmation. At handover, keep the reference and confirm the coverage dates. If the same issue returns, email the shop with the model, symptoms, and reference so we can review it. Read the warranty policy.

    What should I prepare for a device or PCB project?

    For a device, send the exact model, when the fault appears, and any previous work. Back up accessible data and leave passwords out of the form. For a PCB, describe the function, supply voltage, size limits, connectors, and target date. Tell us whether you have a schematic, parts list, design files, or a bare board. We’ll arrange file sharing by reply.

    Can I drop off in Dagupan or mail from another province?

    Yes. Choose local drop-off for an agreed Dagupan meeting point or mail-in for nationwide service via LBC or J&T Express. Wait for packing instructions, the confirmed destination, and courier cost arrangements before shipping. Keep the tracking number with your booking reference.

    Which payment methods can I use?

    Ask for GCash, Maya, or BDO/BPI bank-transfer instructions in your booking correspondence. Confirm the current recipient and amount directly with the shop before transferring. Keep your transfer reference and the shop’s acknowledgment. See payment and cancellation terms.

    How do I get help after handover?

    Reply to your booking email with your reference and describe what has changed. A short clip helps with intermittent faults. Lifetime support lets you ask about the repair even after the warranty window. If additional parts or bench work are needed, we’ll explain the coverage and any cost before proceeding.
