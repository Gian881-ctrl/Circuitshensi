How it works

From first fault
to final test.

Choose local drop-off or nationwide mail-in. We’ll agree on the handover before your device travels.

  1. Submit your request

    Send the model and symptoms. We’ll reply with the next step and arrange a Dagupan handover or shipping via LBC or J&T Express.

  2. Diagnose and quote

    We trace the fault, identify parts, and give you the price and expected turnaround. You decide whether to go ahead.

  3. Repair and test

    After your approval, we complete the agreed work, test the device, and share the result before pickup or return shipping.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book.

How long will my repair take?

Share the device model and symptoms first. We give you a turnaround estimate after assessment, before you authorize the work. Parts availability and courier transit can affect the return date.

How are pricing and diagnostics handled?

A bench diagnostic is ₱300, deductible from the final repair bill. The service cards show starting rates where available. Your quote sets out the work and price for your device.

What warranty and support do I get?

Repairs come with a two-week warranty and lifetime support. Keep your reference and contact us if the repaired issue returns. See the warranty policy.

Can I drop off or ship my device?

Yes. Arrange a Dagupan drop-off with the shop, or request nationwide mail-in via LBC or J&T Express. Wait for the agreed meeting point or shipping address before handing over your device.
