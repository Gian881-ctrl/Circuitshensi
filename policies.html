Terms of service

Quotes and approval

Submitting an inquiry asks the shop to assess your device or project. We’ll confirm the scope, parts, estimated timing, and price with you before starting the agreed work. The ₱300 bench diagnostic fee is deductible from the final repair bill.

Drop-off, mail-in, and payment

Arrange the meeting point or shipping address, packing instructions, and any courier charges with the shop first. Back up your device where possible. Wait for the shop’s payment instructions and keep its written acknowledgment of any transfer.

Changes and cancellation

Email your booking reference to request a change or cancellation. Cancellation before approval is free. Under the shop’s existing policy, a paid repair cancelled within 48 hours of approval carries a 10% holding fee. The shop will confirm the amount, any refund due, and the payment arrangements directly with you.

Warranty policy

Two-week repair warranty

Completed repairs carry a two-week warranty. Keep the service confirmation describing the work and ask the shop to confirm your coverage dates at handover. If the repaired issue returns, email your reference, device model, and a description of the symptoms so the shop can arrange an assessment.

Lifetime support

You can contact Circuit Senshi for help with your repair throughout the device’s lifetime. If further parts or bench work are needed, the shop will explain what the warranty covers and confirm any price before proceeding.

Privacy policy

When you ask for a quote

Circuit Senshi Tech Solutions uses your name, email, phone number, device or project details, symptoms, and handover preference to respond, assess the request, and arrange service. Leave passwords, unlocking codes, and payment credentials out of the form.

Where your inquiry goes

Formspree and EmailJS process inquiry details to deliver the request to the shop and send an email copy when available. The shop handles follow-up in its email inbox. GitHub Pages hosts the site. If you play the embedded video, your browser connects to TikTok.

References on your device

For new requests sent with JavaScript, this browser saves the reference, service name, and submission date for local lookup. Older bookings may contain additional details saved by an earlier version. Clearing this site’s browser data removes its local records. Messages already delivered to the shop are handled separately.

Privacy requests

To ask about, correct, or request removal of inquiry details held by the shop, email Circuit Senshi with the booking reference. The shop will confirm what it can change or delete and any records it needs to retain for service or legal purposes.
